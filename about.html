Who We Are

Pioneering Excellence in
Orthopedic Immobilization

PlastoKast™ is a premier manufacturer specializing in high-performance synthetic casting tapes, pre-padded splint systems, orthopedic bandages, and specialized surgical consumables under our guiding mission to Connect Bones.

Operating under an audited ISO 13485:2016 Quality Management System and CDSCO medical device compliance, our facility utilizes precision resin formulation and cleanroom production lines to ensure consistent setting kinetics, high structural strength, and smooth anatomical lamination.

Developed in close collaboration with orthopedic surgeons, trauma specialists, and cast technicians, our products deliver rapid weight-bearing strength, 100% X-ray radiolucency, and superior patient comfort for modern fracture management and trauma care.

Common Questions

FAQ Desk

Everything you need to know about PlastoKast™ orthopedic casting tapes, splints, kits, and surgical consumables.

Once activated with room temperature water (20°C–25°C) for 2 to 3 seconds, PK Cast™ begins setting within 3 to 5 minutes. It achieves full anatomical rigidity and weight-bearing strength within 20 to 30 minutes, compared to traditional plaster-of-paris casts which require 24 to 48 hours to cure completely.
PK Cast™ (Rigid) provides absolute rigid immobilization for primary fractures, acute bone trauma, and post-surgical stabilization. In contrast, PK Soft Cast™ (Semi-Rigid) provides flexible functional stabilization with soft edges that allow controlled physiological movement, making it ideal for sports medicine, pediatric orthopedics, soft tissue injuries, and removable functional braces.
Yes, 100%. All PlastoKast™ casting products—including PK Cast Graphics™ with colored and cartoon patterns—are completely radiolucent. Orthopedic specialists can clearly visualize bone union, callus formation, and anatomical alignment on standard radiographic X-rays without needing to cut or remove the cast.
The PK Cast™ Kit (available in 4" and 5" widths) is an all-in-one procedural pack engineered for orthopedic clinics and hospital trauma rooms. Each individual kit contains:
  • 2 Rolls of PK Cast™ Fiberglass Casting Bandage (3.6 mtr length)
  • 1 Roll of High-Grade Cast Padding
  • 1 Roll of Tubular Cotton Stockinet
  • 2 Pairs of Nitrile Application Gloves
PK Splint™ consists of a multi-layer fiberglass/polyurethane core pre-padded on both sides with soft, breathable non-woven felt. It requires no additional under-cast padding. Simply cut to the required length from our dispenser box (or select a pre-cut size), activate with water, express excess moisture, mould to the patient's limb, and secure with an elastic bandage. It solidifies in 3 to 5 minutes.
